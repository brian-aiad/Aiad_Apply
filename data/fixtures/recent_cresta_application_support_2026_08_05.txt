About the job
Cresta is hiring an Application Support Engineer for its Customer Success organization. The engineer connects customers with product development, turns complex incidents into actionable solutions, and supports a conversational AI and contact-center platform.

What You'll Do
Investigate, diagnose, and resolve issues involving API integrations, databases, servers, microservices, web hosting, networks, telephony, and containerized applications.
Act as the primary technical contact for customer inquiries through email and a support portal.
Escalate bugs, feature requests, and critical incidents while partnering with Engineering, Product, and Sales.
Create documentation, FAQs, and knowledge base articles for customers and internal teams.
Reproduce customer environments and reported failures to identify root causes and verify fixes.
Develop detailed product and architecture knowledge across common customer use cases.
Identify recurring issues and improve support processes and product quality.
Participate in a rotational on-call schedule for urgent incidents.

What You'll Bring
4+ years of experience in technical support, helpdesk, or customer-facing engineering.
Strong analytical troubleshooting and the ability to deconstruct complex technical problems.
Clear written and verbal communication for technical and non-technical audiences.
Experience with web hosting, HTTP APIs, network architecture, server operating systems, and application log analysis.
Knowledge of VoIP protocols including SIP, SIPREC, H.323, and RTP.
Experience with support ticketing systems such as Zendesk, Linear, or JIRA.
Ability to work independently and cross-functionally in a fast-paced environment.

Bonus Points If You Have
Experience supporting SaaS or CCaaS applications and platforms such as Avaya, Five9, or Genesys.
Experience with AWS services including S3, RDS, or EKS.
Familiarity with Kubernetes and containerized applications.
Scripting experience with Python, PowerShell, or Bash.
Experience with Datadog, Grafana, Groundcover, or comparable logging and monitoring tools.
