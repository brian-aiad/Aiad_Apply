The Manufacturing Engineer supports advanced composite and metallic structures and focuses on stabilizing, sustaining, and improving production processes in a hands-on shop-floor environment.

What You Will Do
Assist and train assemblers in composite fabrication operations including layup, laser projection, ply cutting and kitting, CNC machining, tooling, robotic automation, and bonding processes
Create, maintain, and improve manufacturing documentation such as routings, bills of materials, visual work instructions, and standard work
Ensure manufacturing processes comply with EHS, quality, and engineering specifications
Investigate and resolve production issues using data analysis, root cause methods, and corrective action planning
Use data analytics and SPC tools to improve productivity, First Pass Yield, and operational performance metrics
Participate in PFMEA, control plans, tool design reviews, and continuous improvement activities
Identify opportunities for process optimization, cycle-time reduction, automation, and digital manufacturing solutions
Collaborate with operations, quality, design engineering, and suppliers on daily production support and technical projects
Assist senior engineers in initiatives related to design changes, equipment upgrades, MRB activities, and manufacturing readiness

Qualifications You Must Have
Typically requires a University Degree or equivalent experience and less than 2 years prior relevant experience
Experience interpreting engineering drawings, process specifications, technical reports, and work instructions
Experience with Microsoft Office and project management
Experience with continuous improvement tools such as Lean and Six Sigma
Ability to follow established procedures and adapt equipment or process concepts to production needs

Qualifications We Prefer
Strong analytical, problem-solving, multitasking, and organizational skills
Experience with CATIA V5/V6 or 3DEXPERIENCE and familiarity with Composite Workbench or FiberSim NX
Exposure to autoclave or oven programming, ply cutters, laser systems, or dimensional inspection tools
Knowledge of composite inspection NDI techniques
Experience with high-rate composite manufacturing methods including AFP, RTM, compression molding, or thermoplastics
Hands-on composite fabrication experience through coursework, internships, student projects, or prior shop-floor work
