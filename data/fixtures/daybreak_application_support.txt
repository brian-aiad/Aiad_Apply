Application Support Engineer

Los Angeles, CA · 3 weeks ago
Full-time

About the job
About the Role
The Application Support Engineer partners with Product and Engineering to investigate complex application, data, and integration problems in internal and customer-facing systems. The role resolves immediate incidents, identifies systemic root causes, proposes durable fixes, and improves platform reliability and scalability.

What You'll Do
Investigate customer bugs, outages, employee access, permission, licensing, API integration, event replay, and data synchronization issues.
Review application logs, alerts, and errors with tools such as Sentry and Salesforce.
Troubleshoot web applications across frontend, backend, API, and integration layers.
Correct data-integrity problems, clean records, resolve duplicates, and repair integrations.
Support business applications including Salesforce with exposure to Shopify and Microsoft 365.
Create runbooks and operational documentation and improve monitoring and incident response.
Collaborate with Product, Engineering, and business stakeholders through diagnosis, root cause analysis, resolution, and prevention.

Requirements
Three or more years of experience in application support, production support, or a similar technical function.
Experience troubleshooting APIs, integrations, application logs, and data synchronization.
Ability to diagnose issues across a full software stack and communicate technical findings.
Familiarity with SQL or other data-querying tools is preferred.
