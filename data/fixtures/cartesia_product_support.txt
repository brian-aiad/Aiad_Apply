Product Support Engineer

San Francisco, CA · 2 weeks ago
Full-time

About the job
The Product Support Engineer helps customers succeed with a technical platform by diagnosing high-impact issues and providing hands-on support across APIs, SDKs, partner integrations, and deployment workflows.

Your Impact
Serve as a primary support contact and resolve technical requests through customer communication channels.
Diagnose API errors, SDK behavior, latency, reliability, integration, and deployment issues.
Reproduce reported behavior, perform first-pass incident triage, and write actionable bug reports.
Document solutions in runbooks, customer guides, and internal best-practice material.
Participate in high-priority incident response and an on-call rotation.
Collaborate with Product, Engineering, and customer teams to improve support operations.

What You Bring
Two to eight years in technical support, product support, technical account management, or forward-deployed engineering.
Strong troubleshooting experience with APIs, SDKs, logs, and integrations.
Ability to reproduce and debug errors and collaborate with engineers on resolution.
Clear written and verbal communication for technical and non-technical stakeholders.
Ownership and prioritization skills in a fast-moving team.

Nice to have
Python, JavaScript, or TypeScript scripting for reproducing, mocking, or validating issues.
Familiarity with AI tooling, APIs, or agentic systems.
