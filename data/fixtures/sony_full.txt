About the job
Sony Interactive Entertainment operates PlayStation. GDFT is helping lead the cloud gaming revolution and delivers console-quality gaming experiences.

Technical Operations Support Analyst
The Technical Operations Support team provides frontline operational support for infrastructure, services, and internal processes that ensure reliable delivery of a global cloud gaming platform.

Key Responsibilities
Operational Support:
Acknowledge, triage, and resolve support requests by following Standard Operating Procedures.
Perform routine operational tasks and maintenance while meeting service level agreement commitments.
Escalate incidents and requests outside established procedures.
Maintain platform stability and service availability.
Monitor operational queues and ensure timely resolution.

Documentation & Process Management:
Create and maintain SOPs, knowledge base articles, and operational documentation.
Contribute to process standardization.

Continuous Improvement:
Identify recurring issues, repetitive tasks, and operational inefficiencies.
Recommend automation opportunities and improve workflows and tooling.

Collaboration:
Work with team leads, engineers, and global operational teams.
Communicate ticket status, operational risks, and support activities.

Required Technical Experience (MUST)
1-3 years of Information Technology, Technical Support, or Operations Support experience.
Foundational understanding of computer systems, operating systems, networking, and troubleshooting.
Experience with ticketing systems and service request management.

Preferred Qualifications
Experience with Linux and command-line tools.
Experience troubleshooting hardware, software, or infrastructure.
Familiarity with Jira, Confluence, ServiceNow, or similar platforms.
Exposure to AWS or Google Cloud.
Experience supporting environments governed by SLAs.
Basic PowerShell, Bash, or Python scripting.
Familiarity with monitoring, alerting, or observability tools.
Experience with process improvement.

Please note Sony conducts background checks.
Equal Opportunity Statement
All qualified applicants receive consideration.
