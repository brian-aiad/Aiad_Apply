Product Support Engineer

United States · 11 hours ago · 59 applicants
Remote
Full-time

About the job
This Product Support Engineer role combines customer experience with deep technical problem solving. The engineer owns difficult platform and integration issues, translates technical complexity into actionable customer guidance, and uses recurring support patterns to improve the product and long-term stability.

Accountabilities
Investigate and resolve complex product, API, integration, and data-flow problems.
Provide timely customer support with clear communication from initial diagnosis through resolution.
Apply knowledge of integrations and system architecture to advanced troubleshooting.
Partner with Engineering on escalations, defect findings, and product improvements.
Identify recurring customer issues and recommend process and product changes.
Create troubleshooting guides, technical documentation, and internal knowledge resources.
Share technical expertise to improve support enablement across the organization.

Requirements
Technical background in software troubleshooting, APIs, integrations, and system debugging.
Understanding of internet protocols, web technologies, and data flows between systems.
Ability to explain complex technical concepts to non-technical users.
Experience working with logs, code-level debugging, automation, or scripting is highly valued.
Strong collaboration with Engineering and other cross-functional teams.
Detail-oriented documentation and problem-solving skills in a fast-paced environment.

Benefits
Remote work across the United States and Canada.
