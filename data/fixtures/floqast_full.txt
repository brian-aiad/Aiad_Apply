About the job
As a Technical Support Engineer (Integrations), you will unblock customers facing technical obstacles across FloQast's Accounting Transformation Platform and ecosystem of integrations. This customer-facing role ensures successful integration outcomes by engaging with IT, Financial System Admins, and end users while collaborating with Technical Support, Professional Services, Partnerships, Customer Success, R&D, Sales, Product, and Engineering.

What You'll Do
Own support cases from customers, applying proper prioritization and delivering clear and effective solutions through email and screen share.
Troubleshoot error messages and unexpected behaviors, identifying root causes and sharing technical resolutions.
Perform technical configuration work to optimize customer environments and align with platform best practices.
Handle L1, L2, and L3 support cases focused on integrations including ERPs, cloud storage providers, SSO identity providers, subledgers, and banks.
Use internal admin tools to retrieve and modify account data through CRUD operations.
Use session replay, log analysis, developer tools, and API clients such as Postman for deep-dive troubleshooting.
Contribute to Knowledge Base articles with Product and Education teams.
Participate in standups and Slack discussions while collaborating on complex cases.
Provide feedback on release previews, roadmap direction, and defect prioritization.

What You'll Bring
2+ years of experience in Technical Support, IT, or a related technical field.
Ability to troubleshoot, diagnose, and resolve customer-reported technical issues.
Strong written and verbal communication with technical and non-technical audiences.
Experience working cross-functionally with Engineering, Product, and Customer Success.

Nice To Haves/Other
Experience in a B2B SaaS environment, particularly fintech or enterprise software.
Experience with ticketing systems such as Zendesk, Salesforce, or Jira for case management.
Experience troubleshooting cloud-based platforms, enterprise applications, ERPs, or finance tools.
Familiarity with database querying using SQL, API troubleshooting, and data integration.
Knowledge of authentication protocols including OAuth, SAML, SSO and file transfer mechanisms including SFTP and REST APIs.
Familiarity with log analysis and session replay tools.
Technical writing experience for customer documentation.

The base pay range for this position is $60,000-$90,000.
