About the job
Job Description

Join a dynamic team shaping the tech backbone of our operations, where your expertise fuels seamless system functionality and innovation.

As a Technology Support II team member at JPMorganChase within Healthcare Payments, you will help monitor and support highly available, cloud-hosted software-as-a-service applications that are mission critical. You will help ensure stable transaction processing by investigating exceptions, triaging escalations, and partnering with technology and business teams to restore service quickly and prevent repeat incidents. You will communicate clearly with partners and customers, bring structure to time-sensitive issues, and continuously look for ways to improve monitoring, workflows, and operational processes.

Job Responsibilities

Monitor healthcare transaction processing applications and services to ensure stable, reliable day-to-day performance
Triage issues escalated by service teams and coordinate timely resolution with appropriate partners
Research and remediate processing exceptions to reduce operational risk and customer impact
Use enterprise-authorized AI capabilities to speed incident triage and root-cause hypothesis generation from operational signals while validating outputs and handling operational data according to security requirements
Monitor production application components to support high availability and meet customer service level expectations
Follow operational agreements and runbooks to support end-to-end service delivery
Maintain strong working relationships with key partners and customers through clear, proactive communication
Identify and implement opportunities to improve processes, systems, monitoring, and operational workflows
Produce ad hoc reports using Structured Query Language (SQL) and Oracle databases to support operational and business needs
Apply reuse-first, AI-assisted practices within operational stability routines to identify recurring interruption patterns and support validated remediation actions aligned to resiliency and security expectations

Required Qualifications, Capabilities And Skills

2+ years of applied experience in application operations, production support, or system integration and support
2+ years of applied experience using Structured Query Language (SQL), including querying and reporting against Oracle databases
Working knowledge of enterprise-authorized AI capabilities for production support workflows with strong validation habits and awareness of data sensitivity
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or equivalent practical experience
Ability to read, understand, and apply technical specifications and operational procedures
Ability to review and validate AI-assisted incident recommendations before action, escalating when uncertain and following operational and security expectations
Experience investigating issues using logs, metrics, and other monitoring signals in production environments
Working knowledge of service management practices such as Information Technology Infrastructure Library
Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively across teams
Demonstrated problem-solving skills in time-sensitive, production-impacting scenarios

Preferred Qualifications, Capabilities And Skills

Experience supporting healthcare billing or healthcare management applications
Experience with monitoring and observability tools such as Datadog or Dynatrace
Experience with Linux or Unix in production environments
Experience supporting electronic data interchange workflows
Experience using Jira, ServiceNow, Splunk, or Grafana in an operations context
