This entry-level Product Quality Engineer supports evaluation and disposition of nonconforming material and develops knowledge of Material Review Board processes and quality engineering principles.

What You Will Do
Work safely and follow all safety rules, requirements, procedures, and instructions
Support Material Review Board activities by reviewing and processing nonconforming material under guidance
Assist in evaluating discrepant material and preparing dispositions under established procedures and regulatory standards
Support documentation, investigation, and timely closure of quality notifications
Collaborate with Engineering, Manufacturing, Supplier Quality, and Operations to resolve product and process quality issues
Participate in root cause investigations and corrective action activities
Collect, analyze, and report quality data related to scrap, rework, defect trends, and quality notification aging
Participate in Quality Clinic activities to identify and resolve production quality issues
Assist with continuous improvement initiatives focused on product quality, process capability, and operational efficiency
Support internal audits, compliance activities, and Quality Management System initiatives
Learn and apply quality engineering principles, aerospace quality standards, and company procedures

Qualifications You Must Have
Typically requires a University Degree and less than 2 years prior relevant experience
Must have the ability to obtain and maintain ATF access
Demonstrated analytical and problem-solving skills
Strong written and verbal communication skills and ability to work in a collaborative team environment

Qualifications We Prefer
Bachelor's or Master's degree in Engineering or a related STEM field
Internship, co-op, or academic project experience in manufacturing, quality, or engineering
Exposure to aerospace, defense, or another regulated manufacturing environment
Familiarity with SAP, PLM systems such as Windchill, or other enterprise systems
Basic knowledge of AS9100 and ISO 9001 quality standards
Exposure to Lean, Six Sigma, or other continuous improvement methodologies
Familiarity with engineering drawings, GD&T, or manufacturing processes
