About the job
The Application Support Administrator is responsible for installing, configuring, and maintaining end-to-end production support for enterprise applications, including core banking, loan management, and related products and services. They act as the bridge between end-users and IT teams. Key duties include troubleshooting issues, testing software updates, and creating training materials to keep banking applications running smoothly.

Ensures compliance with established Company policies and procedures and demonstrates responsibility for compliance with state and federal banking regulations, including lending, privacy, Bank Secrecy Act, Anti-Money Laundering, and Customer Information Program requirements.

REQUIRED DUTIES
1. Provides production support for enterprise applications, ensuring stable and reliable operations across all business units.
2. Helps resolve application and technology issues, including triage, root cause analysis, escalation, resolution, and recovery for application, interface, batch, and integration failures.
3. Investigates and resolves Severity 1 critical system-down and Severity 2 major service degradation incidents.
4. Ensures incidents and service requests are resolved within established SLAs, with clear and timely communication to stakeholders.
5. Oversees break-fix activities, coordinating urgent fixes, patches, and production releases as required.
6. Proactively monitors system health, transaction volumes, batch processing, interfaces, file transfers, and user activity to identify and address issues before impact.
7. Performs daily operational hygiene to maintain system stability, including system maintenance, patching, vulnerability remediation, and coordinated application updates.
8. Creates, documents, and maintains application support procedures, runbooks, and standardized operational processes.
9. Maintains comprehensive documentation and institutional knowledge to support operational continuity.
10. Oversees system configuration, maintenance, and implementation of new software releases across multiple platforms.
11. Participates in support execution, testing, and delivery and collaborates with project teams to meet quality, scope, and timeline objectives.
12. Oversees troubleshooting and resolution of complex application, infrastructure, and network-related issues.
13. Keeps current with security and privacy legislation, regulations, advisories, alerts, and vulnerabilities.
14. Researches technology, customer needs, data administration, information security, related services, and FFIEC Guidelines.

MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS
Bachelor's degree from an accredited college or university or equivalent technical education and experience related to information systems management, products, services, or the financial industry.
Minimum of three years of experience in IT and application support, preferably within banking or financial services.
Experience supporting core banking systems and related products, preferably Fiserv core banking platforms.
Background working in regulated financial environments.
Experience with enterprise application support, including SQL and reporting.
Familiarity with ITSM frameworks and ITIL best practices.
Windows server and desktop operating systems, client-server, web-based, and cloud-based applications.
Various database systems management, report writing, application interfaces, and data import and export methodologies.
Working knowledge of production support processes and incident management.
Broad technical proficiency across applications, operating systems, networks, and databases.
Excellent verbal, written, and interpersonal communication skills.
Ability to deal with complex problems involving multiple facets and variables in non-standardized situations.
Ability to work with minimal supervision while effectively managing assigned responsibilities.

Pay $68,400 - $95,600 per year.
